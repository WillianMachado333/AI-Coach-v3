# Understanding Traits, Skills, and Behaviors — 2.2 Understanding Strengths and Risks

_Section 2: Recognizing Personal Tendencies_

## Lesson (course text)

A personal tendency can be helpful in one situation and risky in another. Directness may create clarity but can also create tension.

Notice when a strength becomes overused. Confidence can become dominance, caution can become delay, and attention to detail can slow progress.

Consider how other people experience your behavior. Adjust your approach when your usual style does not fit the situation or support the desired result.

## Framework overview

The same tendency can be helpful in one situation and challenging in another. For example, being highly direct may support clarity but create tension if used without sensitivity.

## Competency Definition

Recognizing strengths and limitations

## Competency Statement

Identifies how personal tendencies can create strengths in some situations and risks or challenges in others.

## Knowledge

- strengths can be overused
- context affects whether a tendency helps
- other people may experience behavior differently
- balanced use improves effectiveness

## Skills

- name a benefit and a risk
- consider impact on others
- identify signs of overuse
- adjust the intensity of a response

## Observable Behaviors

- Describes both benefits and risks
- Notices when a strength is overused
- Invites another perspective
- Adjusts an approach when results are poor

## I Demonstrate This When

- I recognize how my strengths help me succeed.
- I notice when a strength may be overused.
- I consider how my behavior affects others.
- I adjust when my usual approach is not working.

## Performance Indicators

- **Limited**: Needs prompting to demonstrate connect strengths and risks and may apply it inaccurately or inconsistently.
- **Developing**: Sees a tendency as only good or bad.
- **Effective**: Explains both value and risk.
- **Consistent**: Anticipates overuse and adapts early.

## Examples

- Directness improves clarity but may reduce openness if tone is harsh.
- Careful analysis improves quality but may delay a time-sensitive decision.

## Non-Examples

- Avoiding a strength entirely because it once caused difficulty.
- Praising a strength without considering impact.

## Common Misconceptions

- A strength is not effective in every amount or setting.
- Managing a risk does not require rejecting the underlying strength.

## Conversation Starters

- Understanding: Why can the same tendency be a strength in one situation and a risk in another?
- Reflection or Application: When might one of my strengths be working against the outcome I want?
- Practice or Improvement: How can I adjust a strength without losing what makes it useful?
