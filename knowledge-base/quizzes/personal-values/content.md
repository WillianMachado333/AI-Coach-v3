## What the site says about this quiz

Do you ever feel like you're just going through the motions without a real purpose? This Free Personal Values Quiz will help you identify what matters most to you. Once you know your values, you can live more intentionally and purposefully. After taking the quiz, you will receive a personalized report that reveals your values so you can start making decisions based on what's truly important to you.

Personal values are the key to living a meaningful and fulfilling life. They shape our beliefs, decisions, and behavior and even dictate how we interact with others. They are the guiding principles of our lives that help us make decisions, choose relationships, and prioritize our goals. These values usually come from upbringing, culture, religion, experiences, etc. Knowing our values can give us a sense of purpose in life, helping us determine what is important to us and what goals we want to achieve.

How will discovering my values help?

This Personal Values Quiz uses "values theory" to measure your inner drive for the following nine values: achievement, conservation, caring, freedom, respect, tradition, enjoyment, stability, equality, and justice. The quiz asks you to respond to various statements on a five-point scale. After you complete this questionnaire, our system will analyze your responses and provide a personalized guidance report to help you find your values. By helping you understand your core values, you'll gain valuable insights into your purpose, desires, motives, and goals. In addition, it will help you identify opportunities and groups that align with your values and purpose.

Why should I take this quiz?

You might struggle with motivation, boredom, and a lack of satisfaction. A lack of purpose undermines decision making and prevents individuals from feeling passionate about their personal and career goals, and the discontent stems from misalignment with their purpose. Finding your values will help you discover your path and align your activities with what matters most to you. This values quiz can help you discover your purpose or deepen your understanding of the purpose you have already defined for yourself.

When should I take it?

Contemplating your values is an excellent thing to do at any time when you need to make a decision about your life. If you have been asking yourself questions like what are my values or how to figure out your values? Taking this quiz will provide you with the best answer. Identifying and pondering your values will help you gain deeper insights into your life and realize a purpose that will lead you to happiness and contentment.
