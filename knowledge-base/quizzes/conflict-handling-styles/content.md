What is the basis for this quiz?

The conflict handling styles quiz is based on the Thomas-Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument (TKI). It identifies five primary styles of handling conflict: Competing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding, and Accommodating.

These styles are assessed along two dimensions, assertiveness and cooperativeness, providing insights into how individuals approach and manage conflicts.
