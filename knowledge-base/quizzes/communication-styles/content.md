Staying Purposeful During Change

Effective communication is critical in today's fast-paced and ever-evolving world. With the rise of teamwork, remote work, and virtual meetings, it's crucial to understand our unique communication styles to navigate a complex social, professional, and cultural landscape.

Each of us has our distinctive way of communicating, whether through spoken words, body language, or writing. By understanding your unique style, you can enhance your ability to express your thoughts, feelings, and ideas effectively.

How will this quiz help?

Talent Transformation's Communication Styles Quiz provides valuable insights into your preferred communication style. By understanding your unique style, you can identify your strengths and weaknesses in conveying information, knowledge, ideas, opinions, and feelings.

We will use your questionnaire responses to provide personalized guidance on the following communication styles:

Expressive: Characterized by being talkative, conversationally dominant, humorous, and informal. Highly expressive people tend to be fun-loving, informal, happy to participate in conversations, helpful, humorous, and extroverted.

Precise: Thoughtful, concise, substantive, and structured. Precise people structure their communication thoughtfully.

Verbally Aggressive: Characterized by anger, bossiness, contempt, and non-supportiveness. Verbally aggressive people generally talk angrily and loudly, appear authoritative, are poor listeners, and often participate in physical and verbal fights.

Questioning: Unconventional, philosophical, curious, and challenging. People who love to ask questions are generally deep learners, enjoy discovering new information, and tend to criticize and challenge others while in a conversation.

Emotional: Sentimental, dull, tense, and defensive. People with an expressive communication style are generally sentimental and protective and share their emotions openly.

Manipulative: Characterized as ingratiating, charming, mysterious, and concealing. Manipulative people often use deception, self-management, and other methods to impress others; others generally have positive impressions of them.

What is the basis for this quiz?

The Communication Styles Quiz presents carefully crafted questions to identify and evaluate an individual's communication style. The quiz is grounded in research on communication and personality. It considers various factors contributing to an individual's communication style, including verbal and nonverbal cues, tone of voice, listening skills, and overall communication effectiveness.

The quiz aims to help individuals gain insights into their preferred communication style and provide personalized guidance on leveraging their strengths and overcoming their weaknesses in communication.
