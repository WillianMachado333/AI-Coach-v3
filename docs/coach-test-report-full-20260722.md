### Key Observations

1. **Coaching style differentiation: 8/8 coaches maintain distinct styles** across all 3 profiles. The sandwich prompt ordering and persona reinforcement are working effectively.

2. **Profile-aware behavior:** Priya profile (with extensive quiz data) gets coaches that reference completed quizzes and suggest pending ones. Willian and Guest profiles (no quiz data) get coaches that suggest starter quizzes like Talents Identifier and Career Readiness.

3. **Quiz recommendations are contextually appropriate:** Coaches recommend different quizzes for new users (Talents Identifier, Personal Values) vs. experienced users (Conflict Handling Styles, which Priya hasn't taken).

4. **Sarah (Directive) is the most actionable:** She even provided a direct URL to the Career Readiness Quiz for Guest users — the most concrete recommendation across all coaches.

5. **Emma (Nurturing) is the most emotionally attuned:** She consistently focuses on feelings and safety before any recommendations. For Guest users, she went deeper into emotional validation.

6. **No API errors across all 24 test sessions** (8 coaches × 3 profiles). The instruction overflow strategy is stable.

7. **Evan (Exploratory) rarely gives direct quiz recommendations** — instead asks the user what they want to explore. This is by design for the exploratory coaching style.

8. **Guest users get appropriate onboarding:** Coaches don't pretend to know the user's name or history. They suggest foundational quizzes rather than advanced ones.
